Hi there, my name is Tyron and I'm excited to start this blog to share my thoughts and experiences with you. I've always had a passion for fitness, personal growth, and connecting with others, and I hope that this blog will allow me to do just that.

A little bit about me - I grew up in Imus, I finished my Junior High School years at St. Martha Montessori School, then went to Unida Christian Colleges to study TVL-ICT. In my first year I used to have a passion for programming, but I slowly started gaining a passion for business and marketing.

In my free time, I enjoy working out, exploring the city, trying new restaurants, reading and listening to music. I'm also a huge animal lover and have a pet dog named Hunter who keeps me busy, accompanies me always through anything, and makes me feel loved, and entertained.

I'm also the oldest sibling of three, I always try my best to be a good role model to them. We are all very close and rarely have any problems with each other. They are both very smart and hardworking. Their achievements make me feel so proud, I will do my best as the eldest and support them always through happiness and sorrow.

Through this blog, I hope to share my perspectives on a variety of topics, from lifestyle and travel to career and personal growth. I'm looking forward to getting to know all of you and starting this new journey together. Thanks for reading!
