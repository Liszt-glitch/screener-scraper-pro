Gian Fernandez

At just 18 years old, Gian Frances Fernandez has already made a name for herself in the world of music and writing. Born and raised in a small town in the Philippines, she discovered her passion for singing and songwriting at a young age. She started writing songs when she was just 8 years old and hasn't stopped since.

Story

As a singer-songwriter, she has a unique style that blends different genres to create a sound that is entirely her own. Her music is inspired by her personal experiences, and she writes lyrics that are heartfelt (if you are depressed, you can probably relate). Gian's dedication to her craft is evident in the time and effort she puts into her songwriting and performances.

But singing and songwriting aren't Gian's only talents. She's also an avid writer and loves to journal. Her entries are deeply personal and often explore themes of love, loss, and self-discovery. She believes that writing is a way to process her emotions and connect with others.

Apart from that, Gian is always on the lookout for indie shops that sell unique and interesting posters, which she adds to her collection. She won’t admit that it’s an absolute obsession to have sanrio posters, sanrio plushies, sanrio keychains, stickers… Sanrio everything. She doesn't think so (she had a dream of meeting Hello Kitty and dying because of it).
