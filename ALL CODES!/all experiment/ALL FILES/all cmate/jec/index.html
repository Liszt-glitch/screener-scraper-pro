My Top 10 Favorite Foods!

  1. Adobo - a popular dish made with meat, usually chicken or pork, simmered in a marinade of soy sauce, vinegar, and spices.
  2. Sinigang - a sour soup made with tamarind and meat or seafood.
  3. Lechon - whole roasted pig, a staple at celebrations and festivals.
  4. Kare-Kare - a stew made with peanut sauce and meat, usually beef or tripe, and vegetables.
  5. Pancit - a type of noodle dish that can be made with different types of noodles and various meats and vegetables.
  6. Lumpia - a type of spring roll filled with meat and vegetables. Sisig - a dish made with chopped pork, onions, and chili peppers, often served sizzling on a hot plate.
  7. Sisig - a dish made with chopped pork, onions, and chili peppers, often served sizzling on a hot plate.
  8. Caldereta - a stew made with meat, usually beef or goat, and tomato sauce.
  9. Tapsilog - a popular breakfast dish made with marinated beef, garlic fried rice and egg.
  10. Halo-halo - a popular dessert made with shaved ice, evaporated milk, and a variety of sweet beans, fruits and jelly.
